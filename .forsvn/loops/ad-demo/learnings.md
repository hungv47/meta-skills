# Ad Demo — Learnings

> Durable lessons promoted from individual cycle artifacts. Promotion requires high-confidence (`confidence: high` in cycle verdict) AND status = `keep` or `discard` AND lesson reusable beyond this exact creative state AND audience-temp-scoped.

## 2026-05-19 — Cold-traffic subscription audiences respond to concrete-noun hooks over abstract-benefit hooks

- **Cycle:** ad-demo cycle 1
- **Audience-temp:** cold-traffic
- **Evidence:** ROAS 2.4× vs 1.8× baseline (+33% relative) over 14d, $1,650 spend, ~110k impressions, CTR 1.4% vs 0.9% baseline (+56% relative), CPA $18 vs $24 baseline (-25% relative). Confidence: medium (single-cycle synthetic, n=1; pattern needs cycle 3 replication to upgrade to high).
- **Expiry / caveat:** Applies to cold-traffic only — retargeting cycles emphasize urgency + objection-handling and may not lift on concrete-noun hooks. If audience saturation > 30% (lookalike-2% pool > 30% reached), pattern may decay independent of hook quality. Retest hook archetype if subscription price changes > 25% or if creative-fatigue interval drops below 7 days.

> **Provisional learning** — D15.B infrastructure proof. The synthetic cycle 1 was calibrated to exercise the rubric's keep-promotion gate, NOT to establish a real cold-traffic best-practice. Replace this file's contents when migrating to a real campaign loop.
