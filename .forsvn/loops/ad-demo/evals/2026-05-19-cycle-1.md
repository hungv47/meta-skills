## Diagnosis

### Likely Drivers

1. **Concrete-noun hook archetype lifted CTR by +56% relative.** Evidence: CTR 1.4% vs 0.9% baseline; engagement comments shifted from "interesting" (baseline) to "wait — how does it do that?" pattern (concrete-noun cycle), per Meta Ads Manager comments export. Hook framing matches icp-research.md § Cold-Traffic Hooks line 47 ("subscription-app cold audiences scan for specifics, not promises").
2. **Trial-start conversion held despite CTR lift.** Evidence: CPA dropped from $24 to $18 (-25% relative); higher CTR at equal conversion rate means hook qualified clicks more, not just attracted more. Suggests the hook framing pre-qualified the audience rather than inflating the top of funnel.
3. **Hero asset is the next bottleneck.** Evidence: Frequency hit 2.9 (cold-traffic threshold 3.0) — creative still in window but fatigue trajectory steep. CTR slope across week 2 declined -8% (from 1.6% week 1 to 1.4% blended). The hook is doing work; the hero is going stale.

### Confounders

- **iOS-ATT attribution gap (~30%).** Reported ROAS is likely 10-15% understated relative to true ROAS; baseline cycle 0 had the same gap, so cycle-over-cycle comparison is internally consistent but absolute ROAS may be higher than 2.4×.
- **Audience size expansion.** Lookalike-2% pool refreshed mid-cycle (Meta auto-refresh); reach grew from ~28k → ~38k. Could explain part of the CTR lift independent of hook quality.
- **No concurrent campaign cannibalization detected.** Search by parallel campaign launches: none in window.
- **Optimizer learning phase:** completed by day 4 of the cycle (>50 trial-starts achieved); rest of window in stable optimization.
