# Content Demo — Learnings

> Durable lessons promoted from individual cycle artifacts. Promotion requires high-confidence (`confidence: high` in cycle verdict) AND status = `keep` or `discard` AND lesson reusable beyond this exact content piece AND platform/format-scoped.

## 2026-05-19 — LinkedIn carousels for the subscription-app ICP earn save-rate lift when slide 1 names the reader's role

- **Cycle:** content-demo cycle 1
- **Primary-platform:** linkedin
- **Evidence:** save rate 3.1% vs 1.2% baseline (+158% relative) over 7d, ~2,000 reach; saves 62 vs 23 (+170%), shares 18 vs 5 (+260%), comments 14 vs 6 (+133%) — while likes were flat (210 vs 240). Meaningful engagement carried the entire lift; the meaningful-to-vanity ratio rose 3.2× (0.45 vs 0.14). Confidence: medium (single-cycle synthetic, n=1; pattern needs a cycle 3 replication to upgrade to high).
- **Expiry / caveat:** LinkedIn + subscription-app ICP only — the same carousel underperformed on Instagram (save rate 0.9%, non-ICP audience), so the pattern is platform + audience scoped, not a universal content rule. Retest if the ICP role mix shifts or if LinkedIn changes how it surfaces saves. The job-title hook earns the save but did NOT lift click-through — the CTA is a separate, unsolved bottleneck.

> **Provisional learning** — D19 infrastructure proof. The synthetic cycle 1 was calibrated to exercise the rubric's keep-promotion gate and the Engagement-Quality Discrimination dim, NOT to establish a real LinkedIn best-practice.
