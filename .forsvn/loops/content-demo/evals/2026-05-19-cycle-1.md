## Diagnosis

### Likely Drivers

1. **Job-title slide-1 hook lifted save rate by +158% relative.** Evidence: save rate 3.1% vs 1.2% baseline; saves 62 vs 23. The hook names the reader's situation specifically — comment language shifted from generic ("useful") to self-identifying ("this is literally my Tuesday"), per the LinkedIn comments export. Matches icp-research.md § Audience Language ("the ICP scans for themselves before they scan for value").
2. **The lift is meaningful, not vanity.** Evidence: saves +170%, shares +260%, comments +133% — while likes went 240 → 210 (flat/down). Meaningful-to-vanity ratio rose sharply. A save/share is the audience committing the content to later use; the hook earned real intent, not a reflexive tap.
3. **The CTA is the next bottleneck.** Evidence: click-through 0.4% vs 0.5% baseline — the only metric that did not improve. The hook earns the save; the CTA ("Learn more") does not convert the saved attention into a click. The CTA is the going-stale component.

### Engagement-Quality Signals

- meaningful_engagement: saves 62 + shares 18 + comments 14 = 94 (4.7% of reach)
- vanity_engagement: likes 210 (10.5% of reach)
- meaningful_to_vanity_ratio: 0.45 (baseline: 34 meaningful ÷ 240 likes = 0.14) — a 3.2× improvement
- quality_read: strong — meaningful engagement carried the entire lift; the vanity metric was flat
- qualitative_sentiment: positive — comments quoted: "this is literally my Tuesday", "saving this for my next planning block", "the [role] edition framing got me". 14 comments, no tagged-friend padding observed.

### Cross-Platform Context

- instagram (same carousel, cross-posted): save rate 0.9% over ~1,400 reach — underperformed. CONTEXT ONLY — Instagram's audience is not the subscription-app ICP, and a genuine Instagram read would be a separate cycle with `instagram` as the primary platform. Does NOT enter this verdict.

### Confounders

- No LinkedIn algorithm change detected in the window.
- Posting time held constant (Tuesday 9am) vs baseline — not a confounder.
- Follower count stable (no jump that would inflate reach).
- A single comment thread drew 6 of the 14 comments (one commenter replied 4×) — sentiment still reads positive across the distinct commenters; flagged so the comment count is not over-read.

## Next Cycle Recommendation

- Keep: job-title slide-1 hook archetype + carousel format + posting time + audience segment
- Discard: the generic "Learn more" CTA — it under-converts the attention the hook earns
- Watch: save rate — single-cycle synthetic; the job-title hook pattern needs a cycle 3 replication to upgrade confidence from medium → high
- Route next work to: write-social --rev=2 with hypothesis: "CTA-only revision on the LinkedIn carousel; replace 'Learn more' with a specific next-step CTA tied to the slide-1 role framing; hold slide-1 hook + carousel format + body constant; target click-through ≥ 0.8% over a fresh 7d window"
