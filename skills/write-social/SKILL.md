# Write social and community marketing

Produce ready-to-post work that fits the community, actor, and audience. Verify current platform rules
and format limits when they affect the result.

## Establish channel fit

Determine:

- actor: founder, company, employee, customer, or community participant;
- audience and community purpose;
- useful idea, product change, evidence, or question;
- affiliation and disclosure requirements;
- native format and realistic CTA;
- strongest skeptical response.

Recommend not posting when the community-product fit is weak. Never manufacture engagement, votes,
controversy, vulnerability, authority, or customer advocacy.

## Apply the channel

### Product Hunt

Classify the product with a concrete, differentiated tagline. Explain the problem, mechanism, why now,
and invitation for feedback. Make gallery assets tell a progressive product story. Prepare honest
answers on audience, alternatives, pricing, privacy, and roadmap. Measure qualified activation and
objections, not rank alone.

### Reddit

Check the specific community's current rules and norms. Lead with a useful observation, artifact,
result, or honest question. State affiliation plainly. Make the post valuable without the click.
Prefer one well-matched community over cross-posting the same pitch.

### X

Put the claim, observation, or product change in the opening line. Compress wording, not meaning. Use
a thread only when sequence creates cumulative value. Make media carry evidence. Write replies that
advance the idea rather than repeat it.

### LinkedIn

Open on a concrete professional tension, result, or lesson. Prefer lived specificity and real
tradeoffs over inspiration. Keep line breaks readable without theatrical fragmentation. Use founder
voice for conviction and learning; company voice for product truth and proof.

## Deliver

Return the final native post or listing first. Include only the useful supporting pieces:

- alternate hook or title when it tests a genuinely different angle;
- honest disclosure line;
- media or gallery brief;
- reply bank for likely questions and objections;
- sequence timing when multiple posts have distinct roles;
- measurement and a decision date.

Keep publishing and account actions behind explicit approval. If the host cannot guarantee that
boundary, stop with the ready-to-post package.
