# Playbook — Write Longform

[PLAYBOOK] — why this skill exists, philosophy, the anti-collapse boundary, scope, what it pulls from. Loaded on demand.

## Why this skill exists

Longform / pillar content is the SEO and authority moat: the 1500–3000-word piece that ranks, gets cited by answer engines, and becomes the thing a founder links to forever. The stack had `write-copy` (short conversion copy) and `optimize-seo` (search strategy) but no skill that *writes the pillar*. The naive move — point `write-copy` at a long doc — produces exactly the failure this skill exists to prevent: a polished, on-brand, utterly generic essay that restates the first page of search results.

This skill owns the **longform-content discipline**: a strict research → outline → draft → critic pipeline with a structural-quality + **originality** rubric.

## Why this isn't write-copy (the anti-collapse boundary — read this)

The K5 spec's explicit rejection: `write-longform` MUST NOT collapse into `write-copy` + a pack. It earns its existence through three things `write-copy` structurally cannot do:

1. **A research stage that runs FIRST.** `write-copy` writes from a brief. `write-longform` gathers real evidence, maps the consensus (what's already obvious), stress-tests the thesis against its strongest counter-argument, and finds the proprietary angle — *before* any structure exists. A long doc without this stage is generic by construction.
2. **An originality GATE, not a polish pass.** `write-copy`'s rubric scores clarity/persuasion (V/F/U). `write-longform`'s critic has an **Originality dimension with a hard ≥5 floor**, scored against the research agent's named Consensus baseline. A piece that merely restates consensus FAILs regardless of prose quality. This is the dimension that makes the skill not-write-copy.
3. **Section-level structural integrity at length.** `write-copy` optimizes lines and blocks. `write-longform`'s outline agent enforces an argument *spine* — every section advances one thesis, earns the next, and survives a necessity test. This is an argument-architecture discipline that short copy doesn't need.

If a piece could have been produced by `write-copy` on a long prompt, it failed. The critic's Originality floor + the strict research-first order are the structural guarantees against the collapse.

**When it really is just copy:** a 400-word product blurb, a feature announcement, an email — those are `write-copy`. `write-longform` is for pieces that must *argue* something at length and earn citations.

## Philosophy

1. **One thesis, owned and defended.** A pillar argues a non-obvious claim, not surveys a topic.
2. **Research before structure, structure before prose.** The order is the discipline. Drafting first produces filler.
3. **Originality is the product.** The value of a pillar is its delta over the consensus. Measure and gate on it.
4. **Evidence, not adjectives.** Specifics, sources, real examples — or an honest tag. Never an invented statistic.
5. **Every section earns its place.** Length comes from depth, never padding.

## Scope boundary — when NOT to use

| Situation | Use instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| Short conversion copy (headline, CTA, blurb, email) | `write-copy` |
| Social post / thread | `write-social` |
| Keyword research / search strategy / topic map | `optimize-seo` (which may then commission a pillar from this skill) |
| Ad copy | `write-ad` |
| A content calendar / what to publish when | `plan-campaign` |
| AI-tell cleanup on a finished draft | `humanmaxxing` (terminal polish — this skill flags residue for it) |

- **`optimize-seo`** — places the pillar in a topic map.
- **`humanmaxxing`** — terminal AI-tell polish pass on the draft.

## Hard guardrails

- **No invented statistics / fake citations** — instant critic FAIL.
- **Originality ≥5 floor** — the anti-collapse gate; not config-toggleable.
- **No skill count** stated. **No gradient/glow** in any example.
