# X channel

Use for a single post, thread, reply strategy, or launch sequence on X. Verify current platform limits
and features when they affect the deliverable.

- Put the claim, observation, or product change in the opening line.
- Compress wording, not meaning. Preserve the mechanism and proof.
- Use a thread only when the sequence creates cumulative value.
- Make media carry evidence: product behavior, before/after, chart, or concrete artifact.
- Write replies that advance the idea rather than repeat the post.
- Avoid empty suspense, artificial controversy, and “bookmark this” engagement bait.
- Keep links and calls to action proportional to the value delivered in-platform.

Deliver ready-to-post copy, the media brief when needed, and a small reply bank for predictable
questions or objections.
