# Product Hunt channel

Use for a Product Hunt launch or relaunch. Verify current platform rules before advising on timing,
ranking mechanics, incentives, or outreach.

- Lead with the product's change for a recognizable user, not the maker's biography.
- Make the tagline concrete enough to classify the product and specific enough to distinguish it.
- Use the first comment to add context: problem, mechanism, why now, and an honest invitation for
  feedback.
- Make gallery assets tell a progressive product story without requiring captions.
- Prepare replies for likely questions about audience, alternatives, pricing, privacy, and roadmap.
- Coordinate genuine supporters; never script fake engagement, vote trading, or deceptive incentives.
- Treat launch-day traffic as a learning event. Capture objections, qualified visits, activation, and
  follow-through—not rank alone.

Deliver the complete listing copy, maker comment, asset story, response bank, and same-day follow-up.
