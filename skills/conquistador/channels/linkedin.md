# LinkedIn channel

Use for founder, company, or employee posts on LinkedIn. Verify current format constraints when they
matter.

- Open on a concrete tension, result, or lesson that matters to the intended professional audience.
- Deliver the useful idea before asking for attention elsewhere.
- Prefer lived specificity—decision, tradeoff, artifact, number with provenance—over inspiration.
- Keep line breaks readable without turning every sentence into a dramatic fragment.
- Use the founder voice for conviction and learning; use the company voice for product truth and proof.
- Make the call to action natural for the reader's level of intent.
- Do not manufacture confession, controversy, or authority.

Deliver the final post, optional alternate hook when it tests a different angle, and the recommended
first comment only if it adds real context.
