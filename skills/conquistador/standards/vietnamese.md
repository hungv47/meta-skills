# Shared Vietnamese standard

Load this before creating or revising Vietnamese marketing work.

- Declare the intended register: conversational, professional, corporate, editorial, or another named
  context.
- Choose one sustainable self/reader pronoun pair and keep it stable. Change it only when the social
  relationship changes.
- Choose dialect intentionally when regional vocabulary matters; otherwise use broadly natural
  Vietnamese.
- Preserve facts, claims, numbers, product names, and the strength of the original promise.
- Rewrite English calques into Vietnamese sentence logic. Do not preserve English noun stacks,
  passive constructions, or filler transitions merely because they are grammatically possible.
- Keep common loanwords when the audience naturally uses them; explain or translate only when clarity
  improves.
- Prefer the phrasing a real operator would say aloud. Avoid slang inventories, forced particles, and
  theatrical intimacy.
- Read the result end to end for pronoun drift, mixed formality, unnatural rhythm, and accidental claim
  changes.
