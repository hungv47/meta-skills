# Fixture: content improvement

## Request

“This LinkedIn draft is accurate but flat. Make it sound like me and make the product matter.”

## Expected behavior

- Preserve factual claims and the author's recognizable voice.
- Return the revised post first.
- Make the audience moment, mechanism, and consequence specific.
- Explain only the two or three changes that materially improved the work.
- Do not add unsupported metrics, drama, or a generic engagement prompt.
