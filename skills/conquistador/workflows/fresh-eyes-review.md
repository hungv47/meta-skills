# Fresh-eyes review workflow

Use privately before a consequential launch, major strategy, brand system, product brief, or other
artifact where drafting bias could hide a material defect.

Use the relevant creation/evaluation skills—for example `improve-conversion` and `measure-growth`—to
review the artifact against its user outcome, evidence boundary, domain-specific acceptance, and
external-action safety.

Review independently of the drafting rationale. Return a readiness verdict, only blocking/material
defects, what survives, exact required revision, and release boundary. Do not create feedback volume,
rewrite taste, or run unbounded critic rounds. One review and one resolution pass is the default.
