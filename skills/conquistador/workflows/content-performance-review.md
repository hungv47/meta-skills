# Content performance review workflow

Use privately for measured organic text, image, or carousel content.

1. Use `measure-growth` to define one primary platform, source/window, comparable baseline, primary
   outcome, diagnostics, and confounders.
2. Use `improve-conversion` to locate the message-to-action break and design one next-cycle change.

Views, reach, and engagement cannot override failed qualified behavior unless explicitly defined as
the primary outcome. Evaluate one platform/version at a time and finish with keep/drop/change, one
hypothesis, and a stop rule.
