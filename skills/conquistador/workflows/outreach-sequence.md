# Outreach sequence workflow

Use privately for founder, sales, partnership, or other direct outreach.

1. Use `research-positioning` only when the segment, costly moment, proof, or offer is unresolved.
2. Use `write-outreach` for selection signal, finished sequence, reply handling, compliance,
   deliverability, and evaluation.
3. Use `measure-growth` only when results must be compared across cohorts or campaigns.

Prospect from observed demand signals first: active pain, a recent trigger, explicit behavior, or a
posted problem—not title lists. Where no credible demand signal is visible, refresh the signal or
shrink the list instead of lowering standards. Require an observed relevance signal; job title alone
is insufficient. Keep the sequence short, the
ask proportionate, proof honest, opt-out clear, and sensitive material out of unsafe channels. Sending,
enrichment, scheduling, and CRM writes remain behind exact approval.
