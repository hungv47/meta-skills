# Expert decision panel

Use privately and conditionally when a consequential decision has two or more credible positions and
the tradeoff cannot be resolved by a straightforward evidence check.

1. Use `debate-agents` (or `research-positioning` for market substance) to build at least two
   independent, credible positions. Anchor each in product and market evidence and state its premise,
   evidence, upside, downside, risk, and disconfirming condition.
2. Make the positions disagree directly. Test the opposite of any drafting bias or the user's
   preference; agreement is not correctness. Reject sycophantic consensus.
3. Fix explicit decision criteria before resolving—reversibility, downside, evidence strength, or
   value fit. A resolver then chooses; it must not average or split the difference.
4. If criteria and evidence cannot separate the positions, call an honest deadlock, name the smallest
   test or fact that would break it, and give the best bounded choice with the assumption labeled.
5. Offer a multi-agent panel only when the uncertainty is consequential and the host supports
   subagents; never for ordinary drafting or manufactured process.

Keep agents, debate, votes, and internal reasoning private. Return the decision, the criteria used,
and the evidence that would reverse it. A capable single-agent answer remains the fallback when the
host has no subagent support.
