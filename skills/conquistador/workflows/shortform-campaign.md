# Short-form campaign workflow

Use privately for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, founder demos, UGC-style video, and measured recuts.

Use `create-shortform` for current evidence, one audience/hypothesis, hero script, storyboard,
platform-specific recuts, production specification, and evaluation.

When the broader campaign decision is unresolved, use `plan-campaign` first. When real outcome data
must be compared across channels, use `measure-growth` after the platform-specific read.

Do not claim trends from memory, invent UI/VoC/results, or call a crop a recut. Posting, paid
amplification, and external production remain human-owned.
