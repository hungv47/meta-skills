# Knowledge review workflow

Use privately when a decision depends on factual claims, sources, or conflicting evidence.

1. Use `knowledge-review` to clarify the claim, assess each source's authority and freshness,
   estimate uncertainty, and build a contradiction matrix.
2. Resolve conflicts by authority, recency, and causal plausibility, not by counting sources.
3. Use `research-positioning` when the uncertainty changes the market, audience, or position
   decision.

Finish with the resolved position, the surviving unknowns, and a dated recheck trigger so unknowns do
not silently age into facts. This reviews knowledge and factual claims, never code.
