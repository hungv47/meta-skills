# Long-form argument workflow

Use privately when the requested outcome is an essay, article, guide, report, or substantial point of
view.

1. Use `research-positioning` only when audience, category evidence, alternatives, or claim support is
   unresolved.
2. Use `write-longform` to establish the thesis, evidence map, argument, objections, finished draft,
   and claim review.
3. Use `polish-vietnamese` when the finished piece is Vietnamese.

Do not stretch short ideas to a word count or invent evidence to support a thesis. Finish with one
defensible argument, traceable claims, substantive objections, and any fact that needs verification
before publication.
