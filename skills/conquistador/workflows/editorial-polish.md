# Editorial polish workflow

Use privately as the final voice/clarity pass for marketing work.

Use `write-copy` to preserve the author's specific experience, vocabulary, rhythm, claims, and
intent while removing generic framing, repetition, inflated abstraction, manufactured vulnerability,
and unnecessary length.

This is editorial judgment, not a deterministic phrase deny-list or detector-evasion exercise. After
editing, recheck factual claims, quoted language, protected brand terms, and intentional stylistic
choices. Finish with the revised work and only the consequential changes.
