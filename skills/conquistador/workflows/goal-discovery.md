# Goal discovery workflow

Use privately when the user's goal is too broad to choose meaningful work.

Use `research-positioning` to separate known evidence, inference, material unknowns, and the first
decision. Challenge a weak or over-broad premise directly.

Ask at most one bundled question, only when the answer changes the route. Otherwise make the best
bounded assumption and proceed. Finish with a decision contract: audience/job, current evidence,
success signal, exclusions, smallest useful work, and stop/revisit condition.

Do not respond to “grow this” with a generic channel list, 30-day calendar, or visible discovery
ceremony.
