# Pricing and offers workflow

Use privately for pricing, packaging, offer, or discount-strategy decisions.

1. Use `research-positioning` for willingness-to-pay evidence, competitive price anchoring, and who
   the offer must win.
2. Use `knowledge-review` when price, competitor, or market-size claims conflict or rest on thin
   sources.
3. Use `create-paid-campaign` only when an offer must feed a paid test.
4. Use `improve-conversion` only when an existing offer needs diagnosis.

Ground pricing in observed willingness-to-pay and current competitive evidence; do not invent anchors.
Show the discounting and the unit economics that make the offer sustainable. Finish with a
decision-grade recommendation, the evidence that would reverse it, and the exact approval boundary
for any changed price or offer.
