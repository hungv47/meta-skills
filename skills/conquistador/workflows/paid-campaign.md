# Paid campaign workflow

Use privately when the user wants to plan, write, or evaluate paid media.

1. Use `research-positioning` only when segment, costly moment, offer, or proof is unresolved.
2. Use `create-paid-campaign` for the network/segment contract, finished ads, creative direction,
   destination congruence, budget logic, and evaluation.
3. Use `brief-creative` only when a separate production-ready visual/video brief is needed.
4. Use `measure-growth` only when real results need broader causal interpretation.

Ground inputs in current primary sources for auction, pricing, benchmark, and policy evidence; do not
invent targeting precision or performance. Reverse-engineer a credible hypothesis from observed
competitor creative and spend plus existing product evidence before drafting, then let the campaign
test it rather than assert it.

Keep one network, segment, temperature, offer, and destination per test. Verify current policy and
formats. Never spend, upload audiences, or change a live account without exact approval.
