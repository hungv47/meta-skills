# Earned media discovery workflow

Use privately for press, podcast, newsletter, or other earned-media opportunities.

1. Use `research-channel` for the communities, outlets, and people who actually reach your audience.
2. Use `research-positioning` for the story, audience, proof, and angle that make coverage plausible.
3. Use `write-outreach` for signal-led pitches under the outreach standard.
4. Use `measure-growth` only when results need comparison across placements.

Require an observed relevance signal—something the outlet or person has actually covered—before
pitching. Do not invent coverage, fabricate quotes, or offer undisclosed incentives. Keep pitches
personalized and brief. Publishing, seeding, and placed-content agreements stay behind exact approval.
