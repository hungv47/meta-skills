# ICP-to-campaign workflow

Use privately when a product needs an audience, position, and first campaign rather than isolated
copy.

## Required decisions

1. Use `research-positioning` to define evidence boundaries, primary audience, costly moment,
   alternatives, buying conditions, mechanism, proof, and position.
2. Use `plan-campaign` to turn that position into one observable outcome, a focused channel choice,
   a campaign spine, and a small experiment.
3. Use `write-copy` for the primary ready-to-use asset.
4. Use `measure-growth` to define the first real-world signal and stop/revise conditions.

## Finished package

- observed evidence, inference, and assumptions kept separate;
- one primary ICP expressed as selection conditions, not decorative persona detail;
- position with credible mechanism and alternative boundary;
- one campaign spine and reason for the chosen channel;
- finished primary asset;
- sample/window, primary signal, diagnostics, and decision rule.

Do not manufacture customer language, proof, market size, or outcome claims. If evidence is thin,
design the campaign to learn rather than disguise uncertainty.

## Review

Confirm the campaign uses the same audience, costly moment, promise, mechanism, proof, objection, and
action as the position. Reject extra segments and channels that dilute the test.
