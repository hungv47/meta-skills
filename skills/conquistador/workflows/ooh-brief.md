# Out-of-home brief workflow

Use privately for billboards, transit, venue, street, or other physical placements.

Use `brief-creative` to define one communication job, one visual idea, exposure time, viewing
distance/speed, placement conditions, hierarchy, contrast, media-sheet requirements, proofing, and
acceptance.

The primary message must survive a three-second read at real viewing distance. Obtain exact dimensions,
bleed, safe area, color, material, and vendor requirements before final production. Do not depend on a
QR code or fine detail to explain the idea.
