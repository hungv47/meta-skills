# Product Hunt launch workflow

Use privately when the user asks to launch or relaunch a product on Product Hunt.

## Required decisions

1. Use `research-positioning` only when audience, costly moment, promise, mechanism, or proof is
   unresolved.
2. Use `plan-campaign` to define the launch outcome, qualified audience, timing, preparation,
   distribution roles, and decision signal.
3. Use `write-social` for the listing, tagline, maker comment, reply bank, and follow-up.
4. Use `brief-creative` for a gallery story grounded in real or explicitly representative product
   behavior.
5. Use `measure-growth` for qualified activation, objections, and the keep/revise/stop decision.

## Finished package

- strategic bet and evidence boundary;
- tagline, description, maker comment, and listing copy;
- progressive gallery story;
- response bank for audience, alternatives, pricing, privacy, and product limits;
- preparation and same-day sequence;
- primary outcome, diagnostics, observation window, and decision rule.

Verify current Product Hunt rules before advising on timing, ranking, supporters, or incentives.
Never fabricate customers or metrics, script fake engagement, or publish without explicit approval.

## Review

Confirm the product is classifiable, the mechanism is visible, every claim is supported, the gallery
advances one story, and the success signal measures qualified product behavior rather than rank.
