# ICP Refinement — Plugin Operators

## The person

A solo operator running coding agents across several repos. They already
trust the agents to produce; what they do not have is a fast, dignified way
to say *no, with a reason* the agent actually reads.

## The moment

The first `deny` that visibly changes the agent's next artifact. That is
when the loop becomes daily — not at install, not at the first approve.

## Evidence base

- Interviews: 6 operators, 4 running multi-agent setups
- Recurring phrase: "I rubber-stamp because reviewing is friction"
- Counter-signal: two operators preferred terminal-only review — the `--md`
  surface exists for exactly this cohort

## Review Gate

- [ ] The ICP names a person, not a market
- [ ] The activating moment is observable, not aspirational
- [ ] Counter-signals are recorded with their product response
