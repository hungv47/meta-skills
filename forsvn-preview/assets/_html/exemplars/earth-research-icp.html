ICP Refinement — Plugin Operators

Who installs a review-surface plugin, and the moment it earns the daily loop.

The person

A solo operator running coding agents across several repos. They already trust the agents to produce; what they do not have is a fast, dignified way to say no, with a reason the agent actually reads.

The moment

The first deny that visibly changes the agent's next artifact. That is when the loop becomes daily — not at install, not at the first approve.

Evidence base

  • Interviews: 6 operators, 4 running multi-agent setups
  • Recurring phrase: "I rubber-stamp because reviewing is friction"
  • Counter-signal: two operators preferred terminal-only review — the --md
